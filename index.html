FAQ

Common questions, answered.
Where is my data stored? Locally in your browser, and optionally synced across devices with a 6-digit code.
How do I sync a second device? Go to Settings → Device Sync, generate a code, and enter it on your other device.
Can I export my data? Yes — Settings → Your Data → Export, anytime.
